Between visits

Four small habits that save you a big bill.

1

Check tyre pressure monthly

Under-inflated tyres wear faster and quietly push up your fuel bill.

2

Don't ignore dashboard lights

A check-engine or battery light is cheaper to fix early than after it fails on the road.

3

Get the AC checked before monsoon

Fogged-up windows in the rain are usually a gas or filter issue, not a mystery.

4

Stick to the service interval

Most manufacturers recommend every 10,000 km or 12 months, whichever comes first.

Questions

Before you book.

Do I need an account to book a service?

No. You can book a slot without signing up — just save your booking chit, and you can look up or manage any booking later using your mobile number.

Is the price I see final, or can it change?

The amount shown at booking is a starting estimate. If the technician finds extra work is needed once your car is inspected, we call or message you with the revised amount before doing anything — nothing is billed without your go-ahead.

Can I reschedule or cancel after booking?

Yes, any time before your slot from the "My appointments" page — no phone call required, though you're welcome to call if that's easier.

Do you use genuine parts?

Always. Every part is OEM or OEM-equivalent, and the invoice itemises exactly what was fitted.

Do you offer pickup and drop?

For most sevas within central Bengaluru, yes — mention it in the notes field while booking, or call the shop to confirm for your area.
